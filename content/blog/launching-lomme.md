Today I’m launching [Lomme](https://lomme.mattjmcnaughton.com), a programmable
practice band for improvising and comping. Give it a chord progression and it
loops drums, bass, and keys while you play. Adjust the tempo, mix the band, and
follow chord tones on a live guitar fretboard.

Start with one of 100 arrangements or make your own. Everything runs in your
browser, with local saves and importable/exportable arrangements. You can
[try it now](https://lomme.mattjmcnaughton.com) or read the
[source on GitHub](https://github.com/mattjmcnaughton/lomme).
